# Strike Legends: Playtest Kit (Milestone 10)

## 1. Get the game ready (about 5 minutes)

1. In Studio: **File → Publish to Roblox**. The new tracking only runs in the published game.
2. Open **File → Game Settings → Permissions** and set **Playability** to **Friends**.
   Only your Roblox friends can join. Everyone else still can't see the game.
3. If Roblox asks for the content questionnaire, fill it in honestly (a bowling game, no violence, no chat features).
4. Join once yourself from the Roblox app on your phone to make sure it opens.

## 2. Pick your testers

- 3 to 6 friends is plenty. **Mostly phone players**, since that is the main audience.
- Ideally a few who have never seen the game. First impressions are what you want to measure.

## 3. During the test

- **Don't explain anything.** Let them figure it out. Where they get stuck is the most useful thing you'll learn.
- If you can, watch 1 or 2 of them play (in person or on a call with screen share).
- Write down: where they hesitate, what they tap that doesn't work, when they look bored, and what makes them say "whoa".
- Ask them to play for at least 10 minutes, and to come back the next day if they want to (that tests the daily reward and "would they return").

## 4. Questions to ask afterward

1. What did you think the goal of the game was?
2. Was anything confusing or annoying? When?
3. What was the most fun moment?
4. Did strikes feel too hard, too easy, or about right?
5. Did you want to open crates and get new balls? Why or why not?
6. Would you play again tomorrow? What would make you come back?

## 5. Check the numbers (Creator Dashboard)

Go to **create.roblox.com → Creations → Strike Legends → Analytics**. Data can take a few hours to show up.

- **Funnels → Onboarding**: how many players reached each first-time step.
  Steps: Joined → First Throw → First Strike → Double Strike → Opened Crate → Equipped Ball → Ranked Up.
  A big drop between two steps shows where players get lost.
- **Economy**: where coins and gems come from (throws, daily, duplicates) and where they go (crates).
- **Custom events**: Throw (by result: Gutter, Strike...), CrateOpened (by crate and rarity), RankUp, TrialFinished (passed or failed), DailyClaimed.
- **Engagement / Retention** (Roblox tracks these automatically): session length and whether players come back.

## 6. After the test

Tell me what you saw and heard (rough notes are fine). We'll fix the biggest problems first, before any shop or monetization work.
