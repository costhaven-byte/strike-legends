# Stage 3c: every UI string, old and new (for Selim to approve)

2026-11-01. This covers everything a player can see in the rooftop game now (Config.CoreLoop on). Zones 1 to 3, the boss, crates, rebirth and the zone gates are switched off, so their text isn't here.

How to answer: "all OK", or give the numbers you want changed (for example "14: use X instead"). Nothing is changed in the game until you say so.

Voice rules I used: short, plain, like a friend telling you what to do. No hype lines. Labels name the actual thing. No "!" unless something good just happened. Upper or lower case is a looks choice for Stage 3d, so read these for the words only.

**"same"** = I'd keep it as it is.
**"hide"** = this points at something the rooftop game doesn't have. See part B at the end.

## B. Text that points at things the rooftop game doesn't have

These aren't wording problems. The screens behind them don't work in the new loop. I'd hide them for now (code kept, nothing deleted) unless you'd rather keep them.

1. **STREAK / STRIKE TO START** (HUD) and **STREAK / LAST THROW / RANK** (lane scoreboard), **BEST STRIKE STREAK** (leaderboard), **BEST STREAK**, **PERFECTS**, **ON FIRE**, **PERFECTIONIST** (profile). There are no strike streaks or PERFECTs anymore. Hide them, and swap the leaderboard board for "Most coins sold".
2. **Levels, XP and ranks** (section 8, the NEXT LEVEL REWARD card, RANK TRIAL READY!). I checked the code: rooftop throws give no XP. The level bar stays at "LV 1 ROOKIE · 570 XP TO LV 2" forever and the level rewards never pay. Trials also need strikes ("Get 2 strikes in 6 throws"), which only start at ball 9. Pick one: (a) give XP per throw again and turn trials off so ranks go up with levels, or (b) hide the level bar, level rewards and ranks until later. I'd go with (a), because the level rewards are part of the gifts you scaled to ladder income.
3. **Pets**: the only egg on the roof says **SKYLINE EGG / REACH 5 REBIRTHS**, and the pets panel says **PETS UNLOCK IN NEON LANES**. Rebirth is off until Stage 5, so nobody can get a pet. The daily (day 4) and gifts (30 min) hand out a **NEON EGG** you can't hatch ("IT WAITS FOR YOU IN THE NEXT ZONE"). Pick one: (a) open the roof egg now with a coin price, or (b) hide PETS and swap those egg gifts for coins until Stage 5.
4. **Store**: STARTER PACK gives **DUMPSTER DIAMOND**, a crate ball. You always throw your last shop ball, so it does nothing. **NICE PULL!** and **STUCK AT THE WALL?** offers are about crates. **AUTO BOWL** says "every throw is a STRIKE. No PERFECT x2, no streak", which isn't how the rooftop works. **LUCKY / SUPER LUCKY** are crate luck (you're setting them off sale). Fix: swap the starter pack's ball for coins or a bag skip, hide the two crate offers, and change AUTO BOWL to "Throws for you while you stand on your lane".
5. **Spin wheel**: **SECRET BALL!! A LEGENDARY BALL!** and **BRONZE / SILVER CRATE** slices. Turn them into coin and potion slices.
6. **Profile stats and achievements**: CRATES OPENED / CRATE CRACKER, TITAN WINS / TITAN SLAYER, ZONES OPEN / EXPLORER, REBORN. Hide them until those parts are back.
7. **x2 COINS / x2 XP / AUTO pills** on the HUD lead to the store. That's fine. I only list them because they stay as they are.
